What's Killing Our Wildlife

Human activity drives the sixth mass extinction. The causes are interconnected and accelerating — but largely reversible, if we act now.

01

Habitat Destruction

Logging, agriculture, and urban sprawl erase ecosystems at a rate of 27 football fields every minute.

02

Poaching & Illegal Trade

The black market for wildlife is worth $23 billion annually — animals slaughtered for horns, pelts, and scales.

03

Climate Disruption

Shifting seasons and warming habitats outpace evolution. Species that took millennia to adapt cannot survive decades of rapid change.

04

Pollution

Plastic waste, agricultural runoff, and industrial toxins contaminate every ecosystem — from mountain peaks to ocean floors.

05

Human-Wildlife Conflict

As wild spaces shrink, animals venture into human territory. The result is lethal — especially for wildlife.

06

Invasive Species

Species introduced to new environments overwhelm native biodiversity, reshaping entire food webs and erasing endemic life.
