What's Killing Our Wildlife

Human activity drives the sixth mass extinction. The causes are interconnected, accelerating, and largely reversible — if we act now.

01

Habitat Destruction

Logging, agriculture, and relentless urban sprawl erase the ecosystems animals depend on — at a rate of 27 soccer fields per minute.

02

Poaching & Illegal Trade

The black market for wildlife is worth $23 billion annually. Animals are slaughtered for horns, pelts, and scales — living trophies of human greed.

03

Climate Disruption

Shifting seasons, rising seas, and warming habitats outpace evolution. Species that took millennia to adapt cannot survive decades of rapid change.

04

Pollution

Plastic waste, agricultural runoff, and industrial toxins contaminate every ecosystem — from mountain peaks to ocean trenches.

05

Human-Wildlife Conflict

As wild spaces shrink, animals venture into human territory. The result is lethal — for both species, but especially for wildlife.

06

Invasive Species

Species introduced to new environments — by human accident or intent — overwhelm native biodiversity, reshaping entire food webs.
